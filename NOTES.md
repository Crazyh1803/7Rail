# 7Rail: 7 Days to Die x EverRail mashup (working notes)

Status: design agreed, nothing built yet. Continue from here in a session on the PC where both games are installed.

## The idea (agreed with the owner)
In 7 Days to Die you find a stalled EverRail locomotive on a railway that already runs through your world.
You fuel it and ride it through the biomes and past POIs, defend it from zombies, and in the late game extend
its power with 7 Days' own solar panels and gas generators.

- Host game (role "primary"): 7 Days to Die. The player is in this game.
- EverRail (Demo) brings, read from the player's own install at run time (never shipped):
  - locomotive and carriage models and textures
  - train sounds (NOT music)
  - its train rules: fuel, speed, carriages you add and upgrade
- First minute: start 7 Days as normal, find a stalled EverRail locomotive near spawn, fuel it, get it moving.
- Track: preloaded on every world (Navezgane and random-gen), a large ring around the outside of the map, plus
  track switches branching through the different biomes and past POIs. Generated by the mod as chunks load.
- Power: late-game solar carriage and gas generator carriage, built from 7 Days' solar cells and generator parts,
  extending the train's power.
- Players: solo first (v1). Co-op on one train later, through 7 Days' own hosting/joining.

## Melty findings (2026-10-08)
- 7-days-to-die: Unity (Mono). No loader Melty installs, and none is needed: the game loads code mods (DLL with
  Harmony) and XML from {game}/Mods. Launch {game}/7DaysToDie.exe directly (as "20 Minutes to Die" does; one click: yes).
- EverRail is not in Melty's catalog: slug "custom-everrail" (Steam app 4134600, full game). The Demo has its own
  Steam app id, so look for both. Recipe names it role "secondary"; the mod finds the install itself via Steam's
  libraryfolders.vdf and says so in-game when it is missing.
- EverRail is Unreal Engine. Its assets must be converted for Unity. Model to copy: "Transport Fever Sandbox"
  (melty.gg/m/transport-fever-sandbox) ships a converter exe; Melty finds the player's own copy of the secondary
  game, runs the converter once on first Play until a done-file exists, then launches. Plan: converter built on
  CUE4Parse (open source) writes models/sounds/rule values into a cache under {managed}; the 7DTD mod loads it.
- Check first on the PC: whether EverRail's .pak/.utoc files are encrypted. If they are, do not work around it;
  tell the owner and rescope.
- No existing Melty mashup uses EverRail or puts a train in 7 Days. Owner has no Melty drafts yet.
- The Melty connection token is NOT stored here. Get a fresh Publish prompt from Melty Studio for each new session.
